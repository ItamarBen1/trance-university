# Sources

Sources consulted on 2026-11-12 for the history content in `data/trance-data.json` and the lesson text. Wikipedia carries most of the weight; treat dates as approximate.

Not every fact in the dataset traces to this list. See "Known gaps" in `CLAUDE.md`.

- [Trance music (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trance_music): Frankfurt origins, Eye Q and Harthouse, MFS Berlin, Platipus, Bonzai, tempo range, track structure, subgenres, the 2017 revival.
- [Psychedelic trance (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_trance): Goa party timeline, subgenre list, dark psytrance origins, psybient.
- [Goa Gil (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa_Gil)
- [Eyal Barkan (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyal_Barkan): nitzhonot and "Good Morning Israel".
- [Dream trance (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_trance)
- [Children, Robert Miles (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children_(composition))
- [Twisted, Hallucinogen (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_(Hallucinogen_album))
- [Suomisaundi (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suomisaundi)
- [A Split-Second (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Split-Second): new beat origin story.
- [Hardstyle (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardstyle)
- [The Story of Trance, part 2 (Underground Trance)](https://undergroundtrance.com/story-of-trance-pt-2/)
- [Psytrance Guide](https://psytranceguide.com): BPM ranges for psy subgenres.
- [Vini Vici, DJ Mag Top 100 poll 2017](https://djmag.com/top-100-djs/poll-2017-vini-vici): "Free Tibet" remix on the Beatport chart.
- [Zenon Records (SoundCloud)](https://soundcloud.com/Zenon-Records): founded 2003 by Sensient.
- [Parvati Records (Bandcamp)](https://beatspace-parvati.bandcamp.com/merch): started in Denmark, summer 2000.
- [The Story of New Beat (Red Bull Music Academy)](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2018/01/new-beat-feature)
